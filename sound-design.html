Sound Design at Honk Studios

Sound design work covers original audio and soundscapes for visual and interactive media — from theatre productions to installations. Field recorders and ambisonic/binaural microphones support location capture, and Dear VR Pro supports spatial and immersive mixing where a project calls for it.

  • Multi-channel field recorders for location sound
  • Ambisonic microphones and binaural devices for immersive capture
  • Dear VR Pro for spatial/immersive mixing
  • Logic Pro X and iZotope RX for design and cleanup

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of projects do you take on?

Theatre, film, art installations, games and other interactive or experimental work — get in touch with your brief and we'll let you know if it's a fit.

Yes, using Dear VR Pro for spatial and immersive mixing where the project calls for it.
