Creative Projects at Honk Studios

Beyond standard recording and mixing, we host collaborative and experimental audio work — workshops, artist residencies, interactive installations and projects that don't fit a standard studio brief.

  • Ambisonic microphones and binaural devices for immersive/interactive work
  • Dear VR Pro for spatial audio
  • Small-group workshops hosted at the studio, subject to availability
  • Logic Pro X and iZotope RX for production

Supporting Oxford's Creative Community

Honk Studios exists to support Oxford's artists and creative groups, not just standard commercial sessions. If your project needs studio time, space, or audio expertise in a form that doesn't fit our other service pages, get in touch and we'll see how we can help.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of "creative projects" do you mean?

Workshops, residencies, interactive installations, experimental audio — anything that doesn't fit neatly into standard recording or mixing.

Can I run a workshop at the studio?

Yes, subject to availability and the size of your group — message us on WhatsApp with details and we'll confirm what's possible.

Do you offer reduced rates for community or non-profit groups?

Yes — ask us on WhatsApp about your project and we'll see what we can do.

Have an idea that doesn't fit a standard brief?

Tell us about it on WhatsApp — we're happy to discuss unusual projects.
