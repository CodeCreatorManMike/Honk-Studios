Recording at Honk Studios

Honk Studios offers multi-channel recording for vocals, instruments, bands and ensembles in a treated, comfortable room in central Oxford. Sessions are engineered around your project, whether that's a full band tracking together or a solo artist building a song part by part.

  • Focusrite Clarett 8Pre audio interface (multi-channel recording)
  • Multi-channel field recorders for location and ensemble capture
  • Shotgun and lavalier microphones
  • Studio and ambisonic microphones
  • Binaural heads and devices for immersive capture

A Recording Studio in Oxford City Centre

Honk Studios is based at 9 Park End Street, Oxford OX1 1HH — 3 minutes' walk from Oxford train station and 5 minutes from Gloucester Green bus station, with a taxi rank and pay & display parking nearby. That makes booking a session straightforward whether you're travelling from within Oxford or arriving by train from London, Reading or further afield.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own instruments?

Bring your own instruments and amps where possible. Ask us on WhatsApp beforehand if you need to check backline or space for a specific setup.

Can a full band record together, or is it one at a time?

Both are possible — we'll plan the session around your arrangement, mic count and the multi-channel interface capacity.

How do I get a quote for a recording session?

Message us on WhatsApp with your project (band size, number of songs, whether you also need mixing) and we'll confirm rates and availability.

Is the studio easy to reach without a car?

Yes — it's a 3-minute walk from Oxford train station and 5 minutes from Gloucester Green bus station.

Ready to book a recording session?

Tell us about your project on WhatsApp and we'll confirm availability and rates.
