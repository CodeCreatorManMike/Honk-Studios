About Honk Studios

A recording studio in the heart of Oxford, run for the city's artists and creative groups.

Why Honk Studios exists

Honk Studios' aim is to provide facilities and support for artistic endeavours and the wider social benefit of Oxford's artists and creative groups, at rates that keep the studio accessible rather than priced out of reach for local artists and non-profit groups.

We're based at 9 Park End Street, Oxford OX1 1HH — 3 minutes from Oxford train station — offering recording, mixing and editing, sound design, podcast and audio drama production, all from our compact, treated studio room in the city centre.
