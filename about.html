About Honk Studios

A recording studio and rehearsal space in the heart of Oxford, run for the city's artists and creative groups.

Why Honk Studios exists

Honk Studios' aim is to provide facilities and space for artistic endeavours and the wider social benefit of Oxford's artists and creative groups. That's why our rehearsal space rates are tiered — solo, local, associate and commercial — so that Oxford-based artists and non-profit groups aren't priced out of a professional space.

We're based at 9 Park End Street, Oxford OX1 1HH — 3 minutes from Oxford train station — offering recording, mixing and editing, sound design, podcast and audio drama production, and a 5x5m rehearsal/event space for hire.
