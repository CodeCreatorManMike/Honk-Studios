Mixing & Editing at Honk Studios

Once a session is recorded, mixing and editing shape the final result — balancing levels, cleaning up unwanted noise, and editing takes together into a coherent track or episode. Work is done in Logic Pro X with iZotope RX for repair and noise reduction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to have recorded at Honk Studios to get a mix?

No — we can mix files recorded elsewhere. Send us the format and track count on WhatsApp to check compatibility.

Can mixing be done remotely?

Yes, remote mixing sessions are available — useful if you can't get to Oxford in person.

What's the difference between editing and mixing?

Editing is arranging, cutting and cleaning takes (removing noise, tightening timing); mixing is balancing levels, EQ and effects across the whole track. We offer both, together or separately.

Do you offer mastering?

Ask us on WhatsApp about your specific project — we'll confirm what we can offer for mastering or point you toward the right next step.

Have a project that needs mixing?

Message us on WhatsApp with details of your recording and what you need.
