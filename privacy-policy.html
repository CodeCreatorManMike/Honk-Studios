What we collect

We collect information you choose to give us directly:

Advertising cookies, where used, may be provided by Google and other third-party vendors and may be used to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and where required by law we will present a consent option before any non-essential cookie is set.

If and when advertising is active on this site, this section will be updated with the specific provider, consent mechanism, and a link to opt out.

We do not sell your personal information.
