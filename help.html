Help & FAQs

Answers to common questions — if yours isn't here, message us on WhatsApp or email us directly.

How do I book a session or the rehearsal space?

Message us on WhatsApp using any "Chat on WhatsApp" button on this site, or email robin@honkstudios.co.uk. We'll confirm availability, rates and any details we need before your booking is confirmed.

Do you take online payments?

No — this website does not process payments. Rates, deposits and invoicing are arranged directly with us by email or WhatsApp.

What are your rehearsal space rates?

Rates depend on whether you're a solo artist, local artist, associate or commercial hirer, and whether it's before or after 6pm. Full pricing is on the Rehearsal Space page.

What is your cancellation policy?

3 weeks' notice withholds 25% of the booking fee, 2 weeks withholds 50%, and 1 week or less withholds 100%. This applies to all bookings, evening or daytime. Full terms are on our Terms of Use page.

Where is Honk Studios and how do I get there?

9 Park End Street, Oxford, OX1 1HH — 3 minutes from Oxford train station and 5 minutes from Gloucester Green bus station, with a taxi rank and pay & display parking nearby. See the Visit page for a map.

What equipment do you have?

Including a Focusrite Clarett 8Pre interface, multi-channel field recorders, shotgun/lavalier/ambisonic microphones, binaural devices, Logic Pro X and iZotope RX. See the full Equipment page for the complete list.

Something wrong with this website, or a question not answered here?

Email robin@honkstudios.co.uk with details, or use the Contact form.
